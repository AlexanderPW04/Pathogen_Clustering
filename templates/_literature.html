Sprint 4 — AI Metadata Enrichment

Find information in scientific publications that may help improve missing or imprecise collection details (collection place, date or date range, sampling site). Every suggestion keeps its quoted evidence, is checked against the article text, and changes nothing until you approve it. The genomic analyses never depend on this section.

  1. Find publications linked to your samples
  2. Claude reads only the relevant passages
  3. Quotations and the sample link are checked
  4. You approve or reject each suggestion
  5. GeoNames places approved locations; affected date and distance results are recomputed

Uncertainty and limitations

  • A publication being linked to a sample does not mean the article describes that sample's collection site.
  • Model output is checked for exact quotations and for the sample being named, but a quotation can still be misread; every change needs a person's approval.
  • No numerical confidence is shown: model judgments are not calibrated probabilities.
  • City-level GeoNames coordinates are approximate place locations, not specimen coordinates; state-level and county-level areas stay too coarse for the distance cutoff.
  • Date ranges are kept as ranges and do not change exact-day comparisons.
  • This section only looks for sample-collection details; it does not summarize articles, interpret epidemiological trends, or detect outbreaks.
