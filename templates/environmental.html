This page explores previously processed measurements by sample, feature, date, and location. It is descriptive data analysis only; it is not a diagnostic or outbreak-confirmation tool and does not process raw sequences.

Upload processed measurements

Use one row per sample and genomic feature. Required columns: Sample_ID and Feature_ID. Optional columns: Collection_Date, Location_Name, Frequency, Measurement_Value, Coverage, Read_Depth, Quality, and Unit. Frequency uses fractions from 0 to 1. Missing values remain blank.

Files are limited to 3 MB. The organized CSV download is held temporarily for up to 10 minutes.
